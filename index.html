Import Balltime stats (CSV)

In Balltime go to Stats → Export → Spreadsheet (CSV), then upload the file or paste its contents here. The parser finds the header row on its own, matches common columns (player, #, set attempts, assists, set errors, kills, attack attempts/errors, hitting %, serves, aces, serve errors, receptions/pass rating, digs, blocks) without caring about capitalization, and keeps any columns it doesn't recognize as-is.

Court map — where your sets land

Top-down view of the full 18 m × 9 m court, looking from your baseline (bottom) toward the net. Each dot is one logged set, placed at the landing/contact target for its set type (with a little spread so stacked sets stay visible) and colored by quality or outcome. Quicks (1 / 2 / push 1 / back 1) are placed relative to the setter's usual spot (orange marker, right-center). "Other" custom sets aren't placed since their spot isn't known.

3D court — set trajectories

Drag the baby-blue setter around your side of the court, or use W/A/S/D or the arrow keys. Drag anywhere else to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. No sets are drawn until you tap a set type below; tap more to add them, tap again to hide. Each one draws an arc from the setter's hands to where the hitter meets it: quicks stay low, shoot and C ball are medium-fast, and 4 and 5 ball go highest. The 1 ball, 2 ball, push 1, and back 1 move with the setter. Court is 18 × 9 m with a 2.43 m net.
Mouse: drag the setter to move them · drag elsewhere to rotate · scroll to zoom. Touch: drag the setter · one finger elsewhere rotates · pinch zooms. Keyboard: W/A/S/D or arrow keys move the setter (Shift for bigger steps) — ignored while you're typing in a field.
